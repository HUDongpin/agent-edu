## How to record a future written permission

Do not store the permission email, letter, contact details, or other correspondence in the public repository. Retain the immutable original in the approved evidence archive and record only a non-secret internal register or ticket ID plus its SHA-256 digest in the figure manifest. Change a figure from `permission-required` to `written-permission-reviewed` only after an accountable reviewer has completed the scope review below.

The required `permissionClearance` record contains exactly:

- `evidenceReference`: a non-secret internal record or ticket ID, never a public URL to the correspondence;
- `evidenceSha256`: the lowercase SHA-256 of the immutable original permission evidence;
- `grantor`, `grantedOn`, and a human-readable `scope`;
- `expiresOn`: an ISO date, or `null` only when the grant states no expiry;
- `reviewedBy` and `reviewedOn`: the accountable person or role and completed-review date.

The reviewer must confirm, in the retained rights checklist, that the grant covers:

1. the exact source images and the exact served assets bound by the manifest hashes;
2. public display on aicourse.top in all nine course languages;
3. responsive WebP derivatives, approved crops, and any redactions;
4. territory and duration, including whether the grant has no stated expiry;
5. required attribution, trademark, and non-endorsement wording;
6. takedown, revocation, refresh, and asset-substitution conditions.

The pure manifest validator checks the record shape, digest, non-empty fields, and date ordering. The release checker additionally rejects future grant/review dates and expired permission using the Asia/Taipei calendar. If a grant is revoked, expires, becomes ambiguous, or no longer covers the served asset, return the figure to `permission-required`; never preserve release eligibility by weakening the record.
