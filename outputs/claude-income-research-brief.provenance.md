## Real UI capture procedure

The authenticated Claude Desktop application was controlled locally. Before a
capture, its navigation sidebar was collapsed or the image was tightly cropped.
No real client prompt was sent for course content. Opening the New Artifact
surface automatically created a synthetic default request; the resulting
capture is labeled synthetic and is not presented as buyer evidence.

Final masters were converted from temporary JPEG screenshots to PNG using
ImageMagick with `-strip`. Responsive WebP derivatives were created with a
640-pixel and 960-pixel width cap and stripped again. Filenames are cap labels;
the media ledger and renderer use the actual decoded width. Equal-width
derivatives are deduplicated in `srcset`.

The following were removed or excluded:

- account name and avatar;
- conversation and Project names;
- client names and local folder paths;
- usage, spend, plan, and region details;
- private Artifacts;
- browser tabs, credentials, and other applications.

The seven selected surfaces are blank or synthetic Chat, Cowork, tools,
Project, file-output, Artifact, and Skills states. Their source URL, observation
date, dimensions, hashes, alt text, caption, teaching points, privacy status,
and course-authored capture basis are recorded in typed and JSON manifests.

## Change control

Volatile sources must be reopened immediately before release. A correction to a
plan, model, price, product control, term, policy, platform rule, or license is
made in the typed source ledger first, then propagated to lesson copy, figure
caption, assessment, structured data, and release audit. The independent source
verification report is not overwritten; corrections are appended to the
release record with evidence.
