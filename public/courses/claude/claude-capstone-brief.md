## Choose one pathway

Complete one authentic but low-risk workflow in about 120 minutes.

1. Software engineering: make one bounded, tested change in a disposable or public sample repository.
2. Research and data: answer one narrow descriptive question using public sources or a small open dataset.
3. Writing and office work: turn a synthetic source packet into a memo, slide deck, workbook, or other native deliverable.
4. Teaching and learning: create and evaluate a short, grounded activity for a synthetic learner group.

Chat is sufficient for the free path. Research, Cowork, Code, Skills, and Connectors are optional extensions, not requirements.

Suggested domain checks:

- Code: complete diff, focused tests, broader checks, security, accessibility, compatibility, and rollback.
- Research: original sources, claim-to-source fit, quotations, dates, contradictions, and inferential limits.
- Data: units, missing values, transformations, formulas or code, sample recalculation, chart semantics, and reproducibility.
- Writing and office files: factual claims, audience, voice, sources, native rendering, formulas, notes, comments, accessibility, and links.
- Teaching: objective alignment, correctness, learner thinking, difficulty, accessibility, bias, privacy, and assessment boundary.
