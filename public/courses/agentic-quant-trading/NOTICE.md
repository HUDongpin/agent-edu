# Course 17 fixture notice

These files were created for the aicourse.top course “Agentic AI for Quantitative Trading” on 2026-08-26.

- `market-regime-synthetic-v1.csv` is an original, fictional OHLCV time series for the non-existent asset `SYN-A`. Its `evaluation_regime_label` is for retrospective evaluation only: `evaluation_regime_feature_eligible` is always `false`, and the regime is not available to a decision before `evaluation_regime_known_at`.
- `news-signals-synthetic-v1.json` contains original fictional events. It does not describe a real issuer, asset, person, or event.
- The fixture time contract distinguishes `event_at`, `available_at`, `ingested_at`, `known_at`, and `decision_at` (camelCase equivalents are used in the JSON fixture; the bar fixture uses `observed_through` as its event-observation cutoff). A decision-eligible input must satisfy `known_at >= max(available_at, ingested_at)` and `known_at <= decision_at`.
- `risk-policy.template.json` is an original declarative, fail-closed local-synthetic policy template. It records limits and a protected-channel or verified-signature exact-intent human-approval contract, but it does not implement an approval issuer, signature verifier, revocation registry, consumption ledger, or runtime enforcement engine. It permits only the bundled synthetic fixture identity and declares no network access, external paper or sandbox account, broker connection, credential, remote endpoint, market action, or personalised recommendation.
- `fixture-contract-self-test.py` is an original, standard-library-only fixture-contract self-test. It checks local-file integrity, synthetic identity, chronology, the declared timestamp ordering, decision-input availability, selected policy fields, and that performance metrics are not computable from this fixture pack.
- `LICENSE.txt` reproduces the repository MIT licence and HU Dongpin copyright notice; keep it with any redistributed copy of this pack.
- The self-test contains no network-client path, but it does not attest to operating-system-level network isolation. Run it only in the intended local, offline environment.
- The fixture pack does not implement or execute a strategy, order lifecycle, fill model, replay engine, cost model, risk calculation, P&L calculation, performance metric, or reconciliation. It has no external paper/live/sandbox mode, account, credential, endpoint, or market-action capability.
- No third-party screenshot, logo, market dataset, X media, or GitHub repository content is copied into these fixtures.
- The fixtures are distributed under the repository's MIT licence. Linked third-party projects and data providers retain their own licences and terms.

The fixture-contract receipt is illustrative and supports reproducible contract checking only. The pack lacks the return, benchmark or risk-free, position, order/fill, fee/slippage, and portfolio-equity series required to calculate Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, turnover, or net-of-cost performance. Passing the self-test or completing a checklist only makes an artifact eligible for human review; it does not prove no-look-ahead strategy behavior, establish performance, provide investment advice, authorise replay, or authorise any market action.
