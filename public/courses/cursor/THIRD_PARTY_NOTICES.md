## Fail-closed third-party capture policy

A future real product screenshot or other third-party capture must use the separate `third-party-capture` manifest kind. Local availability, public hosting, attribution, commentary, or an unknown licence does not clear republication. Such an asset remains `rights-review-required` and blocks release unless a reviewed `publication-cleared` record binds the exact asset digest, reviewer, review date, legal or licence basis, publication scope, and evidence URL.

No third-party captures are retained by the current Course 4 figure manifest. The former 14 screenshot masters and 28 responsive derivatives were removed after every runtime, test, checker, rights, and provenance reference moved to the original SVG set. Because those deleted files were tracked, repository history remains the recovery mechanism if an internal audit needs to inspect them.
